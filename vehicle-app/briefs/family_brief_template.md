# Vehicle Purchase Project — &lt;FAMILY NAME&gt;

> Template for a new family's `family.md`. Copy this into
> `user-<family-label>/family.md` and have the family fill in each
> section. **Don't edit `family.md` after intake — clarifications and
> research findings go to `user-<family>/research/decisions_log.md`.**

## Family Profile
- Number of adults + children, ages
- Car seat / booster transition expectations
- Occasional overflow capacity needs (carpool kids, in-laws, etc.)
- Living situation — home charging available? garage size?

## Driving Profile
- **Annual kilometres** (best estimate — anchor for the whole TCO model)
- Trip mix (urban-only? regular highway? specific corridor trips?)
- Notable seasonal driving (winter mountain passes, summer FSR, etc.)

## Key Requirements
- Hard requirements (seats, drivetrain, anything truly non-negotiable)
- Strong preferences (cargo, hitch, roof rack, etc.)
- Powertrain openness (ICE / hybrid / PHEV / BEV — which are acceptable)
- If PHEV/HEV, note any preference between dual-drive and series-electric

## Decision Criteria
- What's the primary metric? (TCO over X years is the default)
- Capital cost sensitivity — is there a hard ceiling, or does TCO drive?
- New vs. used openness
- How does the family weight comfort vs. capability vs. cost vs. reliability?

## Geography for Sourcing
- Primary region (where the family lives and shops)
- Acceptable nearby regions
- Anything beyond is "possible but inconvenient"

## Insurance Reference
- Region-specific insurance class / driver factor / no-claims discount
- Current vehicle's premium as a baseline

## Current Vehicle (the do-nothing baseline)
- Make/model/year/km
- Approximate current resale value (the opportunity cost of keeping it)

## Session Notes
- Date intake completed
- Brief delivery mode (mobile chat, in-person, etc.)
- Any open questions to resolve before cohort building begins
