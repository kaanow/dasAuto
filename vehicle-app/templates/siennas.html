How these numbers are computed

All-in price = advertised + mandatory dealer fees (doc, prep, BC Weather Pack on the 2026 quote, etc.) + 5% GST + BC PST (7% under $55k, 8% $55–56k, 9% $56–57k, 10% $57k–125k) + $50 licensing.

Net 5-yr / 10-yr cost = all-in + projected fuel ($1,700/yr) + projected insurance ($1,700/yr) + projected maintenance − estimated retail residual at hold-end.

Maintenance model is sensitive to which Toyota warranty tier (basic 3yr/60k, hybrid system 8yr/160k, traction battery 10yr/240k) is active each year. ~$1,000/yr in-warranty era, ~$2,200/yr when only battery covers, ~$2,700+/yr fully out-of-warranty, +$500 past 200k km, +$300 past age 10.

Residual model: trim-adjusted retail (Limited 1.15×, XSE Tech 1.10×, XSE 1.05×, XLE 1.00×, LE 0.85×) less mileage penalty (10¢ per km over 75k), floor at $5,000.

Assumes 15,000 km/yr driving — anchor for the whole project. If your real number is meaningfully different, fuel and maintenance scale with it. Relative ranking is much more stable than absolute numbers.
