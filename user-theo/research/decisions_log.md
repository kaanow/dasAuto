### Decisions made (no family conflict surfaced)

- **AWD is a scored criterion, not a hard filter** — the brief explicitly
  allows FWD. Applied the selection principle from day one; no AWD-bug
  repeat.
- **Kamloops gas discount** (~10% under Vancouver) applied to gas-burning
  entries; electricity/DCFC/discount unchanged (province-level). No `tco.py`
  change needed — the escalation shape is BC-wide; only the stored anchors
  differ.
- **Insurance** anchored to the family's actual $1,764/yr full-coverage
  quote rather than reconstructing ICBC tables from scratch.
- **Kept honest losers in the cohort** (Bolt EUV, Ioniq 6) to make the
  cargo-vs-efficiency and cheap-vs-capable trades visible rather than
  pre-filtering them out.

### Ranking at launch (10-yr horizon, Theo's weights)

(new), RAV4 Prime (new). Lowest TCO overall is the Bolt EUV (but it ranks
~15th on cargo/corridor/winter). Gas-only CX-5/Outback rank low under the
TCO-primary weighting. The 2018 Corolla baseline lands mid-pack — most
